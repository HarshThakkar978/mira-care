Policy

Privacy & data deletion

Last updated October 23, 2026. Mira Care is a student prototype for tracking perimenopause symptoms and cycle notes, on this website and by texting Mira on WhatsApp.

What we collect

When you text Mira on WhatsApp we receive your WhatsApp phone number, your profile name and the messages you send. Mira saves the notes you share (how you feel, period start dates, symptoms, appointment details and visit questions) plus your recent conversation so it can reply in context. Entries made on this website stay in your own browser.

How it is used

Your messages are processed by Anthropic's Claude API to understand them and write Mira's replies. Your notes are stored in a database hosted by Upstash. Messages are delivered through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

Text DELETE ALL to Mira on WhatsApp at any time to erase everything Mira has saved for your number. For website entries, open Privacy → Delete all local data on the site. You can also email harshthakkar978@gmail.com and we will delete your data.

Not medical advice

Mira helps you organize your own notes. It does not diagnose or recommend treatment. Contact a clinician for medical questions, or emergency services if something feels urgent.
