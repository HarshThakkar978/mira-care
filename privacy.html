Privacy & data deletion

Last updated October 23, 2026. Cura is a student prototype for tracking perimenopause symptoms and cycle notes, in the Cura app and, where available, by texting Cura on WhatsApp.

What we collect

When you chat with Cura we receive the messages you send, and on WhatsApp also your phone number and profile name. In the app your data is linked to a random code stored on your device. Cura saves the notes you share (how you feel, period start dates, symptoms, appointment details and visit questions) plus your recent conversation so it can reply in context. Entries you add on the app screens are saved the same way.

How it is used

Your messages are processed by Anthropic's Claude API to understand them and write Cura's replies. Your notes are stored in a database hosted by Upstash. Messages are delivered through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Community

If you join the Community, the messages you post are seen by the members of your group, under your first name or a nickname, whichever you choose. Claude reads group messages to pick out tips, the Cura team checks those tips before anyone else sees them, and the team can read groups to keep them safe. Tips shown to others never say who shared them.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Me → Erase everything, or text DELETE ALL to Cura at any time to erase everything Cura has saved for your number. Erasing everything in the app also removes your community messages. You can also email harshthakkar978@gmail.com and we will delete your data.

Not medical advice

Cura helps you organize your own notes. It does not diagnose or recommend treatment. Contact a clinician for medical questions, or emergency services if something feels urgent.
